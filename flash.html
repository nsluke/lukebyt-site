Rescue flasher.

Reflash a Tidbyt with the community Tronbyt firmware, right from this page. Afterwards it talks to a server you control — a Tronbyt server on your network, or a LukeByt — instead of a cloud you don't. Plug the Tidbyt into this computer with USB-C and go.

This browser can't reach USB devices. The flasher needs Web Serial, which Safari and Firefox don't implement. Open this page in Chrome or Edge — that's the only requirement.

Your Tidbyt is free.

It just rebooted into Tronbyt firmware. Three steps left, and they happen on your phone:

  1. Unplug the Tidbyt from the computer and plug it into any USB power.
  2. On your phone, join the WiFi network TRONBYT-CONFIG and open http://10.10.0.1.
  3. Enter your home WiFi and the URL of your Tronbyt server — that's what feeds it apps from here on.

Don't have a server yet? Tronbyt server runs on anything that runs Docker, including a $15 Pi. Or reserve a LukeByt — same open ecosystem, server already inside the box.

Troubleshooting
No serial port shows up in the popup
Nine times out of ten it's the cable — swap it for one you've synced data over. Otherwise it's the USB-UART driver: recent macOS and Windows 11 include the CP210x driver, but older systems may need Silicon Labs' VCP driver. On Linux, add yourself to the dialout group.
Connecting or flashing fails partway through
Drop the speed to 115200 baud and try again. Skip USB hubs and plug straight into the computer. If it still fails, unplug the Tidbyt, plug it back in, and reconnect — the ROM loader resets clean every time.
It flashed, but the screen is scrambled or the colors are wrong
That's the panel variant lottery — some Gen 1 units use a different LED panel. Re-flash with Gen 1 (swapped panel). Nothing is broken.
Can I go back to stock Tidbyt firmware?
This page doesn't carry stock firmware, and Tidbyt doesn't publish a restore image. Treat the switch as one-way unless you've dumped your flash first (esptool's read_flash can back it up beforehand).
Is this safe? What are you actually flashing?
The unmodified v1.6.7 release binaries from the open-source tronbyt/firmware-esp32 project, served from this site so your browser can load them. Flashing happens entirely between your browser and the device — nothing is uploaded anywhere.
